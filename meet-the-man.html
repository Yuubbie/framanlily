Meet the Man Behind the Brand

Chineme Francis Ejy-Akwuba

Chineme Francis Ejy-Akwuba
Founder, FRAMANLILY

Every Framanlily development, from the first plot of land to the last coat of paint on a duplex, sits under one name and one standard. That standard starts with Chineme.

From affordable plots to luxury homes, that isn't just a slogan on a flyer. It's the range Chineme built Framanlily to cover.

A company built to go the distance with its clients

Framanlily (RC: 7425007) is a real estate, investment and project development and management company based in Abuja. Under Chineme's leadership, the company has taken on developments across the city, from land banking opportunities in Idu, Lugbe and Kuje for buyers just getting started, to finished duplexes and apartments in Lifecamp and Jabi for families ready to move in.

That range is deliberate. Rather than serving one kind of buyer, Framanlily was built to meet people wherever they are on the journey to owning property and to walk the rest of it with them.

We're still gathering Chineme's own words on his journey and vision for Framanlily. This page will grow to carry that story directly from him.
